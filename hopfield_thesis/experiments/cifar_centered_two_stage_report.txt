====================================================================
INTERPRETATION
====================================================================

  1. The model works correctly on CIFAR after centering.
     N=100: 100% baseline retrieval (down from 20% at N=10 raw).

  2. At N=100, attacks fail — NOT due to confusion but due to basin depth.
     The anti-correlated patterns (cos ≈ -0.01) create deep, well-separated
     energy basins. One pixel (1/1024 dims, Δmax=1.0) cannot overcome the
     entire-pattern similarity signal that anchors retrieval.

  3. At N=500, some attacks succeed. The basins begin to overlap as
     N grows, creating the same exploitable regime seen for MNIST at N=100.
     This confirms the model follows the expected capacity scaling:
     few patterns → robust; many patterns → vulnerable.

  4. The raw CIFAR result (0% attack success) was pseudo-robustness by
     confusion. The centred result at N=100 is genuine robustness by basin
     depth. These are fundamentally different and distinguishable by baseline.
